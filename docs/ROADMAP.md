# Product Roadmap (v8.0 Plans)

## Future Extensions & Directions

### 1. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) Scanner
- Automatically scan screenshots of in-game inventories (weapons, mods list, arcanes) to populate player profiles directly without manual input.

### 2. Live Warframe Market Integration
- Connect to `warframe.market` endpoints to display real-time platinum prices for items and farming routes.

### 3. Advanced AI Voice Advisor
- Support voice queries using local speech-to-text models to allow players to query progression advice hands-free while playing.

### 4. Interactive Star Chart Visualizer
- Map node unlock requirements and display optimized progression pathways directly on an interactive 2D star chart.

### 5. Multi-User Sync Support
- Sync progression checklists and loadout stats across multiple players in the team synergy tab.
